## Hero

Build the company around the product.

Founder Skills gives your coding agent startup workflows for traction, positioning, launch, sales, PMF, and focus — so every session helps you build a successful company, not just a better product.

Install:

```bash
npx --yes github:gvkhosla/founder-skills setup
```

Then ask:

```text
Use co-founder to help me decide what to do next.
```

## Core idea

The hard part is not only building.

Entrepreneurial people already know the product matters. Founder Skills helps with everything around it: who it is for, why they care, how you reach them, what signal matters, and what to do next.

## What it helps with

- Find sharper customers
- Choose smaller bets
- Launch with a point of view
- Read traction honestly

1. Start with `co-founder`.
2. Run the right workflow.
3. Close with `compound`.
